== Workflow 2: Data-Driven Provisioning (Performance Insights)

**The Goal:** Determine the exact hardware required to meet a specific Service Level Agreement (SLA).
**The Value:** Preventing expensive over-provisioning and avoiding under-resourced pod crashes.

[NOTE]
.Simulation 2: Sizing the Hardware
====
* **Click through the simulation below.**
* Open the **Performance Insights** tab for a validated model.
* Adjust the **Max Latency** and **Min RPS** sliders. Watch how hardware configurations (like the H200 or A100) that fail to meet these thresholds are instantly hidden, removing the guesswork from capacity planning.

== Workflow 3: The Guardrailed Deployment

**The Goal:** Deploy the selected model to the cluster without causing an outage or selecting the wrong runtime.
**The Value:** Protecting cluster uptime and enforcing infrastructure guardrails.

[NOTE]
.Simulation 3: Deploying the Model
====
* **Click through the simulation below.**

* Pay attention to the **Deployment Strategy** selection. You must explicitly choose between a **Rolling update** (zero downtime, requires overlapping GPU capacity) or **Recreate** (brief downtime, saves GPU resources).
