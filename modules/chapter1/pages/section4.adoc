== Key Observations

As you clicked through the simulation, did you notice how the UI enforces operational guardrails? Here is the backend reality of what you just experienced:

=== 1. The "Provider" Filter 
* **Observation:** In the sidebar, you saw options for different providers, tasks, and licenses.
* **The Backend Reality:** Each of these checkboxes corresponds to specific metadata labels in the catalog sources. This allows administrators to seamlessly blend third-party validated models with their own internal, custom-trained models pulled from external storage.

=== 2. The Model Card "Source"
* **Observation:** When you opened a Red Hat validated model card, the "Source" field displayed an OCI registry path.
* **The Backend Reality:** This confirms the asset is delivered as a **ModelCar** (OCI artifact). It will be cached locally on the node, enabling near-instant scaling and avoiding brittle, massive file downloads. 

=== 3. Data-Driven Evaluation (Performance Insights)
* **Observation:** The **Performance Insights** tab provided detailed benchmark data for specific hardware types.
* **The Backend Reality:** Instead of guessing how a model will perform, users can filter the data by:
  * **TTFT (Time to First Token) & TPS (Tokens Per Second):** To ensure chatbot responsiveness and overall throughput capabilities.
  * **E2E & ITL:** To understand total end-to-end request latency and inter-token speeds.
  * **SLA Enforcement Sliders:** By setting the maximum acceptable latency or minimum RPS sliders, users instantly hide hardware configurations that fail to meet their Service Level Agreements. 

=== 4. Intelligent Runtime & Hardware Matching
* **Observation:** In the Deployment Wizard, the "Hardware Profile" and "Serving Runtime" were intelligently filtered or auto-selected.
* **The Backend Reality:** The system analyzes the model framework and your available hardware profiles to automatically recommend the best serving runtime. If your cluster lacks the required GPUs to run a specific model, it prevents deployment—protecting you from a pending pod failure.

=== 5. Deployment Strategies (Rolling vs. Recreate)
* **Observation:** The wizard required a choice between "Rolling update" and "Recreate" deployment strategies.
* **The Backend Reality:** This puts uptime controls directly in the UI. **Rolling updates** ensure zero downtime by starting new inference pods before terminating old ones. **Recreate** saves resources by terminating old pods first—a crucial guardrail when a cluster is tightly constrained on expensive GPU resources and cannot support overlapping compute capacity.
